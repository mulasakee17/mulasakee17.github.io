*Thesis

Intelligence is moving out of chat windows — into tools, devices and the physical world. The interesting questions now live at those interfaces.

We are interested in how agents acquire capabilities, how they act on real objects, and how collectives of agents decide. Interfaces and infrastructure for those questions are what we build.

Research asks what changes.
Building discovers what works.
