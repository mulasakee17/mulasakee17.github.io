*Research Direction

Constraint Migration

Technological progress may change not only what a system can do, but which constraint limits it. As technologies relax different constraints at different rates, previously secondary bottlenecks can become binding.

SwarmAlpha

A controlled multi-agent experimental setting for studying these dynamics.

Phase Reversal

A macro application: how AI may reorder the binding constraints of capital allocation.
