Moonblade

Static object context interface

A classmate’s experience assembling a drone at summer camp raised a question: how could AI obtain reliable context for that particular device?

I began with the idea of giving physical objects an AI interface. The current Object Context tools organize information about a particular object so AI can read it for a task. The replay alongside this text comes from an earlier Runtime experiment.

SwarmAlpha

Multi-agent decision research

SwarmAlpha began with financial prediction and multi-agent collaboration. It became a study of how information changes agents’ reported judgments.

The research archive contains the experiment system, actual model runs, and findings that changed my questions. Active research has stopped; the code and records remain available.

Read the case

Information disclosure can change a group’s reported judgment.

Experiments compared what agents reported after receiving different information. The results prompted further work on how to describe and measure the group’s changing state.

The Ancestors

Personal atlas of human questions · Public reading path

When I’m unsure what to think about a question in my own life, I want to know how others have faced it before.

I’m slowly building a private home for those encounters: the people, their works, and the arguments that make me reconsider how I want to live. One reading path is open here.

Read the case

Am I building the life I want—or the life others have taught me to admire?

A life can look successful from the outside and still leave its owner unsure. Read with Adam Smith, Harry Frankfurt, and John Stuart Mill: what deserves admiration, which desires to stand behind, and what choosing for oneself requires.

Follow the question

Question the first answer.

I like large questions. Building something small is often how I discover what I misunderstood about them.

A design can change, an experiment can disappoint, and a book can make a familiar question feel strange again. I want the work to leave room for that.

I choose the questions and key designs, and take responsibility for what I make of the results.

About

My interests run from Physical AI and agent systems to philosophy and complex systems. I enjoy finding connections between fields, then seeing what happens when I try to build with them.

I work extensively with AI on research and implementation, while continuing to strengthen my own engineering foundations. These projects are part of that learning.

Earlier work includes Personal Agent Runtime, which separates a model’s proposed action from permission and verification, and the Phase Reversal Hypothesis. RIZHICHI remains accessible as a secondary page.
