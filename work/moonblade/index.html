Understanding a particular object.

How can the next person or AI inherit what was understood about a physical object—and know when that understanding needs to be checked again?

Moonblade · Engineering prototype

What exists

Moonblade is a software prototype for binding object-specific context and skills to an interaction, with checks and evidence around the decision. Its replay module reads an evidence sequence and reports sessions, state transitions, contradictions, and events.

This shifts the focus from an AI that can describe equipment to a record that another operator can inspect. Replay exists; complete persistent object memory, cross-model inheritance, and cross-device repair are not established by it.

Role and choices

I set the object-grounded question, system boundaries, and acceptance criteria; AI supports implementation, analysis, and checks. The design choice emphasized here is to preserve evidence that can be read again, rather than treating a model’s fluent answer as sufficient authority to act.

The record must tell a later reader what was observed, what the system permitted, and what remains to be verified. The case does not claim that the entire inheritance direction is already implemented.

The next useful check

A real task must test whether the inherited context remains useful after the object changes, whether stale assumptions are detected, and whether another operator can recover the relevant evidence. The current software exhibit cannot answer those physical-world questions.
